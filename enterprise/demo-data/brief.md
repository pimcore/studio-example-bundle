# Design brief: Thunder Loop

**Client:** Pimcore Kingdom
**Designer:** Loopwerk Engineering
**Ride:** Thunder Loop, a family thrill coaster for the park's new "Storm Valley" area

All names in this brief are fictional.

## Company statement

We at Pimcore Kingdom stand for safety first, honest craft, guests of every age and rides that last for decades.

What we expect of a supplier:

- **Safety limits are hard limits.** Every design stays within the brief's limits, the 45 m height first.
- **Every change is reviewed.** No change to the track goes ahead before the park has reviewed and approved it.
- **Be open about revisions.** Each revision says what changed and why, the moves away from the approved one too.
- **One shared record.** Designs and their revisions are shared in this portal, not by mail.

## Target audience

Families and teenagers from 10 years up, first-time looping riders included. Minimum rider height 1.20 m. The ride
should feel bold but never brutal: two inversions at most, short airtime, no long sustained forces.

## Requirements

- **Maximum height: 45 m.** No point of the track may be higher than 45 m above the station; the lift hill is the
  highest point. The park's building permit for Storm Valley allows no more.
- One or two vertical loops, the first one the ride's signature moment, visible from the area's main path.
- Capacity: one train of 6 cars with 4 seats each (24 riders), about 600 riders per hour.
- The ride starts and ends in the same station, with a brake run before it.
- Footprint within the Storm Valley plot; a helix or turn may be used to bring the track back to the station.

## Approval

Every revision of the design is checked against this brief before the park approves it. The approved baseline is
revision 2.
