# About the park

Pimcore Kingdom is a theme park built around one idea: thrills with care. Every ride should make a guest's heart race and bring them back to the platform smiling, from the first-time rider to the coaster fan who has seen them all.

**Safety first.** No ride opens before it has met the park's standards, and no schedule or budget comes before them. A ride that is not safe is not finished.

**Craft that lasts.** We build rides to run for decades. We value careful engineering, honest materials and designs that age well over the newest effect.

**Partnership.** Our rides are designed with suppliers, not just bought from them. We share our briefs early, answer questions quickly and review every revision together, in this portal.

All names on this page are fictional.
